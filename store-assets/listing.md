## Full Description (4000 characters max)
FieldLens is the all-in-one field operations platform built for contractors, construction teams, and field service businesses.

**Manage Your Entire Field Operation from One App**

From work order dispatch to crew tracking to on-site inspections — FieldLens gives field managers and workers a single source of truth, whether they're in the office or miles off the grid.

**Key Features:**

📋 **Work Order Management**
Create, assign, and track work orders from creation to completion. Attach photos, notes, and documents. Get real-time status updates as your crew checks in and out.

👷 **Crew & Scheduling**
Schedule crews by skill set, location, and availability. Track real-time crew locations on the map. Manage certifications and equipment assignments from one dashboard.

🔍 **Mobile Inspections**
Conduct field inspections with customizable checklists. Capture photos, annotate findings, and generate PDF inspection reports — all from your phone.

📍 **GPS Job Tracking**
Track crew arrival and departure times with GPS check-in. Monitor active job sites on a live map. Automatically log time for payroll and billing.

📡 **Works Offline**
No internet on the job site? No problem. FieldLens works fully offline and syncs data automatically when you reconnect.

📊 **Real-Time Reporting**
Track job progress, crew productivity, and material usage. Export reports for clients and billing.

**Who Uses FieldLens?**
- General contractors and specialty subcontractors
- Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC companies
- Property management and maintenance teams
- Environmental and infrastructure inspection firms
